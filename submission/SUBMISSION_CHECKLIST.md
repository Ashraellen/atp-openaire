## Deadline safety

The official hackathon page currently shows two different times for 20 August 2026: **18:00 CET** in one section and **23:59 CET** in the detailed timeline. Operational rule: treat **18:00 CET as the deadline** and submit earlier.

## Final manual check immediately before submission

- [ ] Open the repository while logged out/private browsing and confirm all submission links are public.
- [ ] Confirm the latest `ATP smoke test` run is green.
- [ ] Confirm `README.md`, `submission/STORY_FINAL.md`, `submission/REGISTRATION_TEXT.md`, and `LICENSE.md` render correctly on GitHub.
- [ ] Confirm core ATP repository and DOI links resolve.
- [ ] Copy registration fields from `submission/REGISTRATION_TEXT.md` without changing the prior-work distinction.
- [ ] Save screenshot/PDF or confirmation email after form submission.

## Do not do before submission

- Do not publish private MONOLITH masters or internal canon.
- Do not describe the OpenAIRE extension as the origin of ATP.
- Do not claim that research retrieval automatically determines literary choices.
- Do not claim a proprietary model is required; the method is model-agnostic.
- Do not wait for the disputed final-hour deadline.
